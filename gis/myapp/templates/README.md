# Template structure

- `base.html`: layout goc cho giao dien nguoi dung.
- `pages/`: cac trang noi dung tong quan nhu trang chu va gioi thieu.
- `news/`: trang danh sach va chi tiet tin tuc.
- `catalog/`: danh muc san pham va trang chi tiet thuoc.
- `locations/`: ban do GIS va chi tiet chi nhanh.
- `shop/`: gio hang, thanh toan va cac luong mua hang nhanh.
- `account/`: dang nhap, dang ky, ho so, lich su don va tra hang.
- `admin_panel/`: giao dien quan tri, da tach tiep theo tung nhom chuc nang.
- `registration/`, `errors/`, `emails/`, `includes/`: cac nhom he thong da co san.
